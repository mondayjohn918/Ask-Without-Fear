Community Guidelines

  • Be kind and respectful. Disagree gently. No mocking, insults or put-downs.
  • No judgement. Everyone is here to learn. No question is silly.
  • Protect privacy. Never share someone else's personal details or story without their permission.
  • Stay rooted in Scripture. Share what the Bible says, and be clear when something is your own opinion.
  • Keep it safe and clean. No bullying, explicit content, or pressure on anyone.
  • Speak up if something is wrong. If someone is being hurt or is in danger, tell a trusted leader straight away.
