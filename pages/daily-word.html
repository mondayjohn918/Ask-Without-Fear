Daily Word

A short verse and reflection to start your day with God.

Today's Word

Have not I commanded thee? Be strong and of a good courage; be not afraid, neither be thou dismayed: for the LORD thy God is with thee whithersoever thou goest.

Joshua 1:9 (KJV)

Reflect

Courage in the Bible is not the absence of fear. It is choosing to keep going because God is with you. Think of one thing you have been avoiding because of fear, and ask what taking one small step might look like today.

Pray

Lord, thank you that I am not alone. Give me courage for today, and help me to trust you in the things I am afraid of. Amen.
